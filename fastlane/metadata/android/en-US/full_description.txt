ABook Player is a native, privacy-focused, and open-source audiobook player designed for Android, tailored for high-quality audio, elegance, and reliability.

It supports both modern .abook container archives and standard audiobook folder structures (including automatic merging of multi-disc CD1/CD2 directories).

Key Features:
• Modern Material 3 UI: Warm paper-style design for the library and true OLED black (#000000) for the player screen to maximize battery savings.
• Media3 Audio Engine: Smooth background playback, Android Auto integration, and rich system lockscreen/notification controls.
• Smart Folder & Disc Merging: Automatically scans multi-disc setups into single continuous audiobooks.
• Metadata & Cover Art Fetcher: Search Audible and iTunes with multiple candidate matching and high-definition cover previews.
• Reliable Progress & Chapter Sync: Automatic position saving, anti-loss protection, and dynamic chapter recovery.
• Sleep Timer & Shake to Extend: Sleep timer with chapter-end support; shaking the device automatically extends playback by 15 minutes.
• Local Listening Statistics: Daily and weekly listening streaks and time graphs — 100% offline and tracker-free.
• Car Mode & Widgets: High-visibility large button interface for driving and homescreen playback widgets.
• Broad Format Compatibility: Plays FLAC, MP3, M4A, AAC, and OGG.
• 100% Free & Open Source: No advertisements, no telemetry, no proprietary analytics.
